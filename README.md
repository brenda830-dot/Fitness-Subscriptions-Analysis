## Customer Cohort Analysis

Question: After how many months do customers typically cancel?
Customers experience their biggest decline in retention around Month 3
### Findings

- [Month index with the largest drop, and the retention % before and after]
The largest drop occurs between Month 2 and Month 3.
Retention decreases from approximately 78% in Month 2 to 50% in Month 3.
This represents a significant decline of approximately 28 percentage points.
- [How later months compare: gradual or sudden?]
  After Month 3, retention declines more gradually.
  Customers who remain beyond the first three Months tend to continue generating transactions.
  This suggests that the first three Months are the most critical period for customer retention.
- [Any difference between 2024 and 2025 cohorts, as retention %, not raw counts]

### Conclusion

[One or two sentences: when to act on churn]
Customer retention is strongest during the first two months, followed by a significant 
decline around Month 3. The business should focus on customer engagement, onboarding and 
retention campaigns during the first 60-90 days to reduce cancellations and improve customer lifetime value.
